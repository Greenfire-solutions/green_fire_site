Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Green Fire (greenfire.earth) is run by Jordan Larsen. This page explains what information we collect, why, and what we do with it. In short: we only collect what you choose to give us, we use it to work with you, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • What you type into our forms. When you sign up, book a session or ask about a project, we keep your name, email, and anything else you choose to add, such as a phone number, your business name, your website, and your answers to our questions.
  • Payments. Payments are handled by Stripe. We see what you bought and that it was paid. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach us.
  • Small settings in your browser. Some pages remember simple choices on your own device, such as where you left off. This stays in your browser and is not sent to us.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell any information about you.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, run the classes and projects you sign up for, and deliver what you bought.
  • To send you things you asked for, such as a class link or a download.

Who else sees it

Only the services that run this site and our work: Vercel (hosting), Stripe (payments), and Google (email and file storage). Each handles it under its own privacy terms. We share your information with nobody else, unless the law requires it.

Our Google Drive connection

Green Fire runs a small internal tool, "Emerald Dream Drive," that connects to Google Drive. It is used only by Jordan, on his own Google account, to upload, find and share his own files. It does not ask for, read or store anyone else's Google data. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while we are working together and for our records afterwards. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop hearing from us. Email civilizationexplorer@gmail.com and we will take care of it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Questions? Write to civilizationexplorer@gmail.com.
